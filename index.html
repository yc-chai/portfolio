About

I used to be a marine truck driver and thought I would stay in that role for life. However, the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 challenged everyone, including me. During a period of forced leave, I applied to Republic Polytechnic and enrolled. From the start, I discovered deep passion for IT technologies and programming, eager to understand the techniques behind application development and algorithms. This passion has brought me to where I am today.

My main focus these day is providing solutions and demonstrations for potential customer at Oracle. I most enjoy addressing client pain points by building well-designed and highly extensible applications. In my free time, I explore new technologies and tools through small experiments from websites to enhance my career skillset.

Currently, I plan to further my studies with the goal of becoming a full stack developer. I am seeking opportunities to broaden my horizons and gain experience and skills, both through university and beyond. Let's find out how I can solve your pain points.
